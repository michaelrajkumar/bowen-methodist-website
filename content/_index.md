## Welcome to Bowen Memorial Methodist Church

<div class="visitor-notice" style="background-color: #fff3cd; border: 2px solid #856404; border-radius: 8px; padding: 20px; margin: 20px 0;">

<h3 style="color: #856404; margin-top: 0; padding-left: 0; border-left: none; background: none;">Important Notice for Visitors</h3>

<p><strong>Troublemakers, stay away.</strong> No one is forcing/enticing you in any manner to come to church.</p>

<p>Other non-members can come to church only of their own free will. No one is forcing/enticing you in any manner to come either.</p>

<p><strong>If you do choose to come by your own free will:</strong></p>

<p>a. You must mandatorily print the <a href="/images/consent-form.pdf">self-declaration form</a>, fill it, sign it, and bring it with your ID for verification - <strong>every time you visit the church, not just on Sundays</strong></p>

<p>b. If you are visiting as a group/family, ensure that you add all the names and IDs on the form, and all must sign the form.</p>

<p>c. You are prohibited from using your phone or any other device to record the service - in part or in full.</p>

<p>d. You must maintain the decorum and sanctity of the church.</p>

<p><em>Harsh diktats such as the <a href="https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/maharashtra/2026/Bill20of2026MH.pdf" target="_blank">Maharashtra Freedom of Religion Act, 2026</a> require harsh measures to protect the church and its true worshippers.</em></p>

<p><strong>Members, we know who you are - please walk in :-)</strong></p>

<p>Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.</p>

</div>
